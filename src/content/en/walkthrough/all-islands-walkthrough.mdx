This walkthrough covers the full How to Fish progression: five islands, every boss summon, and the purchases that actually matter. It is written for a solo run — the route stays viable in co-op, but the boss notes flag where a solo player carries all the risk. Followed cleanly, the whole run lands under an hour, which also lines up with the Bean achievement's one-hour clear window.

## Speedrun Ground Rules

Five habits save the most time across a run:

1. **Skip the grill unlock.** Volcano lava on Island 5 cooks everything for free — do not spend coins on the barbecue.
2. **Finish kills with hip-fire.** Unscoped finishing shots raise the sell multiplier, which is the fastest money engine in the game.
3. **Drop dead weight.** Useless items get discarded immediately; the radar lives on the boat, not in your backpack.
4. **Respect the two danger spikes.** The Albatross on Island 4 and the double whale fight on Island 5 cause most failed runs.
5. **Track time from the main menu.** Under 60 minutes also earns the Bean achievement.

## Island 1 — Lighthouse Island

Do not fish here at all. Dig clams, keep digging, and stop at **60 coins**.

Buy order: **knife ($45)**, **crab rod ($3)**, and a **beer ($12)**. Knife upgrades can wait — the base knife is enough.

Summon the **Spider Crab** boss by handing the $12 beer bottle to the captain NPC sitting in the chair, then fishing with the empty bottle he hands back. In the fight, low health is fixed by grabbing more clams. Once the crab dies, give its shell to the lighthouse keeper to receive the **radar**, then set sail.

## Island 2 — Forest Island

Two goals here: a **pistol** and one extra backpack slot. Dynamite is the shortcut — one or two blasts on a school of fish covers the ~$50 you need. Park the radar on the boat before you shop, and ditch the knife once the pistol is in hand.

For the boss, collect **three leeches** from the ground spawns and hand them to the woman at the cabin NPC to receive leech bait. That summons the **Giant Piranha** — an easy pistol kill. Deliver the fish bone back to her and she unlocks the Island 3 coordinates.

## Island 3 — Desert Island

This is the shopping island. Buy the **SMG** — it carries the rest of the game — plus an extended magazine and bullet damage upgrades. **Extended mag first**, then damage; 27-28 damage per bullet is enough, no need to max it.

Money tip: end kills with hip-fire for the higher sell multiplier, and sell everything. For the boss, catch up an **endangered rare fish** and trade it to the tourist NPC under the seaside tree for the carrot bait, which summons the **Pufferfish**.

Blue Shark warning: if one shows up before you own the SMG, reset the fight by dying — the fight is not worth the risk early. With an SMG in hand, kill it and sell it, but **do not hand the shark in** for the grill unlock; the speedrun route skips the barbecue entirely.

## Island 4 — Rock Island

Shopping list: **SMG compensator ($600)** and the tuna boss bait (**~$1,200 total**). Keep the backpack trimmed to SMG plus rod, and stock healing food — throw dynamite at a school before the fight and keep the catch.

The **Albatross** is summoned by dropping the defeated tuna's corpse on open ground. Do not rush the pickup: grabbing the tuna spawns the bird immediately, so heal up first. If a seagull steals the corpse, just drop it again.

The fight itself is cover play: most damage goes out from beside the weapon shed, and you duck inside when the Albatross dives or does its ranged drop attack. This is the run's first real skill check — repeated wipes here are a signal to restart the clock. Keep the **boss meat**; it becomes food on Island 5.

Checkpoint: arriving on Island 4 at **30-32 minutes** means the pace is on track.

## Island 5 — Lava Island (Volcano Military Base)

Endgame shopping: **SMG silencer**, at least one **science bait**, and maxed bullet damage. Red dot sight is optional, based on aim confidence.

Sequence:

1. **Cook the albatross meat** by dropping it into the volcano lava — the free-grilling shortcut.
2. Hand dynamite-caught fish to the scientist NPC to receive the **whale summon item**.
3. Summon and kill the **Bowhead Whale** (phase one). After the kill, discard the fishing rod to free backpack space, then grill snails from the ground for extra healing stock.
4. Carry the whale corpse up the rotating plank path on the right side of the volcano and **drop it into the crater lava**. That summons the true final boss: the **Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale**.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/fish/bowhead-whale-final-boss-2.webp" alt="The mutant bowhead whale rising out of volcano lava in How to Fish" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>Phase two — the Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale, the game's true final boss.</figcaption></div>

Failure budget: across both whale fights you can afford **one death total**. Two consecutive wipes mean the run is better restarted. On the win, grab the **whale tail** — do not lose it, and definitely do not grill it — hand it to the yellow-suited scientist for the **speedboat key**, board the boat at the shore, and the ending cinematic rolls. Run complete.
