## How to Fish on Steam

**How to Fish** is available on **Steam** (APP ID: 4001890), developed and published by **Dazed Games**.

You can find the game on its [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4001890/How_to_Fish/).

## Launch Offer

At launch, How to Fish ran an introductory offer with a **38% discount**, which ended on **August 27, 2026**. The current price may differ — check the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4001890/How_to_Fish/) for the latest pricing and any active sales.
