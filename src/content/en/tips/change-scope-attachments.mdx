Out of the box, How to Fish binds **Z and C** to cycle scopes — and also to switching weapon skins, which is how you end up changing your rifle's paint job mid-firefight. The fix takes one minute in the settings menu. The catch: the game has no in-scope zoom adjustment, so "changing your scope" means swapping the whole attachment.

## The Default Problem

Z and C cycle **installed scopes** (Z for previous, C for next) — and they are also the weapon skin switching keys. In a fight, one missed press flips your skin instead of your sight. Since kills finished while hip-firing pay a higher sell multiplier than scoped kills, the scope keys matter less than you would think — but when you need a specific attachment, the conflict gets weapons killed.

## How to Rebind the Scope Keys

1. Press **ESC** → open **Settings** → go to the **Controls** tab.
2. Scroll down to **Switch Scope Up** and **Switch Scope Down**.
3. Select each and bind what you want — **mouse side buttons, G, V**, whatever sits clear of your combat keys.
4. Save.

That is the whole fix. Side mouse buttons are the popular choice since scope swapping then never touches your movement cluster.

## What You Cannot Do

There is **no per-scope magnification control**. Scopes swap as complete attachments — red dot, compensator, suppressor-style sets — and you cannot tune zoom inside one. If the aim feels wrong, the answer is swapping to a different attachment, not looking for a zoom slider that does not exist.

## Worth Knowing for Fights

The game's money engine actually rewards **not** aiming: finishing kills from the hip raises the sell multiplier, which is why the [speedrun route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) treats scopes as optional comfort. Rebind the keys, keep the compensator for the Albatross, and aim only when the fight demands it.
