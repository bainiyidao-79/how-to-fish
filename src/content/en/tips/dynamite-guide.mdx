Dynamite is How to Fish's most honest item: you give a fish shop $25, and the ocean gives back dinner. One blast typically surfaces 4-5 ready-to-collect fish, which makes it the fastest food source in the game — and, in the [speedrun route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough), the fastest way to hit an island's shopping money.

## Where to Buy Dynamite

The **Island 2 (Forest Island) shop** sells dynamite at **$25 per stick**. Sail the lighthouse route in and look for the shop building — no quest unlocks it, and it does not require finishing any Island 4 bird content first. It is available the moment you can afford it.

Short on cash? Sell regular fish or knock out a shop quest first; dynamite pays itself back quickly once you learn the throw.

## What One Blast Yields

A single explosion generally kills and surfaces **4-5 fish**. The exact haul varies with school density, throw placement, and the current game version — treat 4-5 as the norm, not a guarantee.

Three facts to respect:

- **One stick, one blast.** Dynamite is a single-use consumable; thrown is spent.
- **Nothing floats? Diagnose before re-throwing.** Either the throw landed short or the fish were destroyed outright — do not chain-throw into the same empty water.
- **It is food, not an income scheme.** For pure profit, rod fishing with hip-fire kills and cooking beats blasting.

## How to Throw It Without Killing Yourself

Dynamite damages the thrower too, and the game's physics are happy to prove it:

1. **Equip the dynamite, aim at the school, press throw.**
2. **Move immediately after releasing** — backward or sideways, out of blast radius. Do not stand at the landing point admiring the explosion.
3. **Extra care from a boat:** the camera and the boat's drift can leave you standing right at the blast point. Check your escape space *before* throwing.

Wait for the blast to fully resolve, then walk the shoreline collecting the floated catch.

## When Dynamite Is Worth It

Buy it when the goal is **fast food for the road** — $25 for several catches is efficient pre-boss stocking, and the walkthrough route uses it exactly that way before the Island 4 and Island 5 fights. Skip it when the goal is saving money: rods are free per cast, and the sell prices on cooked fish beat the blast yield over a session.

One more thing dynamite does well: two of the game's achievement hunts route through it — the [Everyone's dream](/achievements/everyones-dream-achievement) seagull kill and the [I'm the bird now](/achievements/im-the-bird-now-achievement) boat launch both start at the same $25 shop counter.
