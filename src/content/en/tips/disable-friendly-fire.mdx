How to Fish co-op runs on a physics engine that does not care whose boat you are standing on — friendly fire is real, and a mistimed shot deletes a friend's health bar as happily as a fish's. Whether you can turn it off depends on three things: the lobby settings, the MOD list, and the game mode. Check them in that order.

## Method 1 — Check the Room / Lobby Settings

In multiplayer, friendly fire is usually a lobby option, not a global one:

1. The **host** opens the room creation or room management screen.
2. Look under **Game Settings** or **Room Options** for a **Friendly Fire** (or PVP) toggle.
3. Switch it off — damage between players stops applying in that lobby.

Only the host can change it, so if the toggle is missing or locked, it is a host conversation, not a settings hunt on your end.

## Method 2 — Audit Your MODs

MODs can force or break the game's damage rules in ways that look like unfixable friendly fire:

- **Peace / no-death / god-mode MODs** can block damage rules at the engine level — including changing how player damage applies.
- Check the game folder for **BepInEx/plugins** or a **Mods** directory; remove anything you did not consciously install, then restart the game.
- **Host tool MODs** sometimes add commands (like /players status or damage controls) — if the lobby runs one, the host should check its documentation before touching base-game settings.

Reminder: for a clean vanilla session, everyone uninstalls MODs — mismatched MOD lists also cause [version mismatch errors](/multiplayer/version-mismatch-fix), so the cleanup does double duty.

## Method 3 — Confirm the Game Mode

Some co-op modes keep player-vs-player damage enabled by design, while the casual/co-op default suppresses it. If your lobby has friendly fire that will not turn off, check which mode the host actually launched — switching to the standard co-op mode typically restores the no-friendly-damage default.

## When You Cannot Turn It Off

If the current session refuses every toggle, workarounds that work tonight:

- **Spread out on the boat** before fights — blast radius discipline beats settings.
- Adopt the [dynamite handling rules](/tips/dynamite-guide): clear escape space before every throw.
- Rebuild the lobby fresh — some session states stick until the room is recreated.
