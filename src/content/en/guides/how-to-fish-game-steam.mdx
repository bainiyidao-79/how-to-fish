How to Fish lives on Steam, and that is the only place to get it. Dazed Games self-publishes the title as a PC release, so buying it directly from the [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4001890/How_to_Fish/) is the whole story — no deluxe editions, no launcher detours.

## Launch Pricing

The release opened with a **38% introductory discount** that ran until **August 27, 2026**. That offer has since ended, so the current price is the standard list price — check the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4001890/How_to_Fish/) for live pricing and any seasonal sale.

## Worth Knowing Before You Buy

- The game is built around **online co-op for up to four players**, but it is fully playable solo — our [speedrun walkthrough](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) is written from a solo run.
- Progress is session-based: five islands, a boss at each one, and a boat to upgrade along the way.
