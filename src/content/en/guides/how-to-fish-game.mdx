How to Fish looks like a cozy fishing trip until you actually play it. The game opens with your crew drinking on a boat, crashing into a small island, and discovering that the only way home is to master every part of the fishing business — catching, killing, selling, and occasionally blowing fish up with dynamite.

## The Premise

**How to Fish** is a **1-4 player physics based fishing simulator** developed and published by **Dazed Games**. The setup is deliberately absurd: while drinking and boating, you suddenly crash into a small island. To work your way back home, you have to learn How to Fish — literally.

## What You Actually Do

The Steam page lays out the loop, and it goes well beyond casting a line:

- **Fish fish, kill fish, sell fish** — everything you pull out of the water can be fought, cooked, and sold
- Start with no gear or weapons, then earn money for better rods, guns, and boat upgrades
- Complete quests and fight bosses to unlock new islands, each with more dangerous creatures and better gear
- Hunt the rare variant of every fish species
- Land trick shots for bonus money
- Gamble your earnings for even more money

## Islands, Bosses, and a Very Long Way Home

Progress runs across five islands — Lighthouse, Forest, Desert, Rock, and Lava — each with its own fish species, mini-bosses, and a main boss that guards the route to the next island. The final sequence pits you against the Bowhead Whale and its mutated form, and it is the only way to earn the speedboat key that ends the game.
