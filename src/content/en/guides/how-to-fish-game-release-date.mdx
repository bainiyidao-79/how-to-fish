**How to Fish** released on **Steam on August 20, 2026**, self-published by **Dazed Games** for PC. There was no early access phase and no console version at launch — the game arrived as a finished 1-4 player co-op package.

## How the Launch Went

The release landed hard. Within roughly a week, How to Fish peaked above **268,000 concurrent players** and stacked up a **91.67% positive rating** from thousands of Steam reviews — numbers most fishing games never see. We put the full picture, including what the reviews praise and complain about, in our [launch reception breakdown](/guides/200k-concurrent-players).

## What Released on Day One

- Five islands — Lighthouse, Forest, Desert, Rock, and Lava — each with its own fish pool and boss
- A full boss route ending with the Bowhead Whale and its mutated lava form
- Online co-op for 1-4 players, plus a solo-viable run for speedrunners
- Weapons, boat upgrades, a casino, and the rare-fish hunt on top of standard fishing
