## About the Game at Launch

How to Fish launched as a **1-4 player physics based fishing simulator**. The official Steam description:

> A 1-4 player physics based fishing simulator. While drinking and boating you suddenly crash into a small island. To work your way back home you have to learn How to Fish.

The game launched with an introductory discount of 38% off as a launch promotion.
