How to Fish hands you a knife and expects you to be grateful. A few islands later you are carrying automatic weapons, and the question stops being "can I fight this fish" and becomes "which gun ends it fastest." The answer has two parts: the **assault rifle** owns the damage record, and the **SMG** owns everything else.

## Assault Rifle — Highest Damage in the Game

On raw numbers, nothing beats the assault rifle. Fully upgraded bullet damage pushes a single shot to **100 damage**, which is the hardest-hitting shot any weapon in the game produces.

That makes it the pick for deleting tanky targets — island bosses and the rare heavy spawns — especially in co-op, where one player dedicated to rifle duty can carry the damage check while friends handle bait and cleanup.

## SMG — The Actual Speedrun Weapon

The SMG is the backbone of any fast run. High fire rate with respectable damage makes it the most reliable boss-killing tool in the game, and it is affordable the moment you reach **Island 3 (Desert Island)** on the [all-islands route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough).

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/weapons/best-weapons-1.webp" alt="The SMG held up against the sky in How to Fish" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The SMG — affordable on Island 3, relevant until the credits.</figcaption></div>

The upgrade order matters more than the gun itself:

1. **Extended magazine first** — downtime between reloads costs more fights than raw damage does
2. **Bullet damage second** — 27-28 per bullet is the sweet spot; fully maxing it is optional, not required
3. **Compensator ($600, Island 4)** — accuracy for the Albatross fight, where every miss matters
4. **Silencer (Island 5)** — the final comfort slot before the double whale fight

## Pistol, Knife, and Everything Before

The **pistol** (Island 2) is a bridge purchase: cheap, adequate for the Giant Piranha, and obsolete the moment the SMG exists. The starting **knife** is a similar story — it handles the Spider Crab on Island 1 with zero upgrades, then gets discarded the first time you can afford real firepower.

## Which Should You Buy?

Both, in sequence. The SMG first — it is the weapon you will actually shoot 90% of the time, and the [speedrun route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) is built around its upgrade tree. The assault rifle is the late-game damage trophy: buy it when money stops being a problem and point it at the thing with the biggest health bar.
