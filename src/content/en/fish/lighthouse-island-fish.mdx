Lighthouse Island is where every run starts, and it keeps things deliberately small: four common species, no rare fish, no mini-boss, and a single gatekeeper boss standing between you and the boat. Of the game's **49 fish species across five islands**, this island holds the entry-level four.

## Bait

Lighthouse Island fish bite on the **sausage bait**. Stock it before you cast — nothing on this island responds to the other bait types.

## Fish Species

- Brown Crab
- Rock Crab
- Lobster
- Shrimp

**Rare fish:** none. The rare-variant hunt starts on later islands, so do not waste time here chasing one.

**Mini-boss:** none. Island 1 has exactly one boss fight on the way out.

## Boss — Spider Crab

The Spider Crab is the island's exit exam, and its summon is the game's first NPC puzzle:

1. Buy a **beer ($12)** from the shop.
2. Give the beer bottle to the **captain NPC** sitting in the chair.
3. Take the empty bottle he hands back, and fish with it — the Spider Crab takes the bait.

Fight notes: the crab hits hard for a first boss, and a solo player heals by digging **clams** between exchanges — the island's other resource doubles as your pharmacy. Once it dies, carry the shell to the **lighthouse keeper** to receive the **radar**, which unlocks the route to Forest Island.
