Killing the Bowhead Whale feels like the ending. It is not — it is the recruitment step for the real final boss. How to Fish's true finale asks you to weaponize your own kill, cook your way through a two-phase whale war, and deliver one specific body part to the right scientist without grilling it by accident.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/fish/bowhead-whale-final-boss.webp" alt="The bowhead whale surfacing beside the player's boat in How to Fish" loading="eager" /><figcaption>The Bowhead Whale — phase one of the game's two-stage finale.</figcaption></div>

## Step 1 — Summon the Bowhead Whale

The summon item comes from the **yellow-masked NPC** on Lava Island: hand over **five of any fish** (ground snails appear to count; seagulls apparently do not) and receive the **fish bait bucket**. Cast it, and the Bowhead Whale surfaces for phase one.

## Step 2 — Prepare Before the Volcano

After the whale dies, the prep window decides the final fight:

- **Top up ammo** — every slot.
- **Drop the fishing rod.** Backpack space is healing capacity now.
- **Grill everything.** Snails picked off the ground and spare fish go straight into the volcano lava; stockpile the cooked heals.

## Step 3 — The Volcano Summon

Carry the whale corpse up the **rotating plank path on the volcano's right side**, reach the crater, and **throw the corpse into the lava**. The Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale — the game's true final boss — rises out of the caldera.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/fish/bowhead-whale-final-boss-1.webp" alt="The volcano crater glowing on Lava Island in How to Fish" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The crater — drop point for the whale corpse, birthplace of the final boss.</figcaption></div>

## Step 4 — Kill the Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale

The mutated whale fights harder and hits bigger, but the shape of the fight is the same: keep moving, unload the [upgraded SMG](/weapons/best-weapons), and eat grilled stock between exchanges. The realistic failure budget across both whale fights is **one death** — two consecutive wipes usually mean the run's economy is behind, and a restart is cheaper than another summon cycle.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/fish/bowhead-whale-final-boss-2.webp" alt="The mutant bowhead whale thrashing in the volcano lava in How to Fish" loading="lazy" /><figcaption>The Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale — the last health bar between you and home.</figcaption></div>

## Step 5 — Whale Tail, Scientist, Speedboat

On the kill, the whale drops the **whale tail**. Two rules from every failed run ever recorded:

- **Do not lose it.**
- **Do not throw it in the lava.** It is a quest item, not food.

Carry the tail to the **yellow-suited scientist NPC** and trade it for the **speedboat key**. Board the speedboat at the shore, interact to start it, and the ending cinematic plays. That is the actual credits — the crew finally goes home.
