Forest Island is where How to Fish opens up. The species count jumps from four to ten, mini-bosses appear for the first time, and the island's boss — the Giant Piranha — is gated behind an NPC trade instead of a shop purchase.

## Bait

Forest Island fish respond to the **beginner bait** — the cheapest tier, which fits the island's role as the early-game fishing ground.

## Fish Species

- Piranha
- Alligator Gar
- Mackerel
- Cod
- Spiny Ray
- Pike
- Salmon
- Perch
- Triggerfish
- Goldfish

**Rare fish:** none. Like Lighthouse Island, the rare-variant hunt does not start here.

## Mini-Bosses

Two mini-bosses patrol the island's waters:

- **Ocean Sunfish**
- **Great Pike**

Both are worth killing on sight — mini-boss kills pay well, and the sell money feeds the Island 2 shopping list (pistol and an extra backpack slot) in the [main route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough).

## Boss — Giant Piranha

The Giant Piranha does not spawn from a shop item. The summon runs through a gather quest:

1. Collect **three leeches** from the ground spawns around the island.
2. Hand them to the **straw-hat girl NPC** to receive **leech bait**.
3. Cast the leech bait — the Giant Piranha answers.

The fight itself is the first real gun check. The standard route buys a **pistol** on this island, and the piranha dies cleanly to it — no upgrades strictly required. Delivering the fish bone back to the NPC unlocks the **Island 3 coordinates**.
