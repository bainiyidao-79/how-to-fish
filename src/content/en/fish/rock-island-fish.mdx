Rock Island raises the stakes across the board: eight common species, two rares, a mini-boss that requires careful timing, and the Albatross — the fight the community rates as the first genuine run-killer.

## Bait

Rock Island fish respond to the **orange pro bait** — the professional tier, fitting the island's position as the second-to-last stop.

## Fish Species

- Bass
- Eel
- Red Snapper
- Skinless Catfish
- Flounder
- Tiger Fish
- Flying Fish
- Parrotfish

**Rare fish:**

- Pixel Fish
- Walking Fish

Both rares are collector targets for the completion log — and Rock Island is the first place the rare hunt meaningfully overlaps with the main route.

## Mini-Boss — Tuna

The Tuna is not just a mini-boss; it is the key to the island. The shop sells the **tuna boss bait (~$1,200 total)**, and catching the tuna triggers the Albatross window. The critical detail: **do not pick up the tuna's corpse immediately**. Grabbing it spawns the Albatross right away, which is a great way to start the hardest fight on the island while unprepared.

Need healing mid-window? Pick the tuna up briefly, heal, then drop it again — the spawn only fires when you commit.

## Boss — Albatross

The summon is physical: **drop the defeated tuna's corpse on open ground**, and the Albatross comes for it. One warning the source guide hammers — **seagulls can steal the corpse**. If that happens, the summon fizzles; just drop the tuna again.

The fight pattern:

- The Albatross **dives** and uses a **ranged drop attack**.
- Both are answered by the **weapon shed**: fight from beside it, duck inside when the bird commits to a dive or a bomb run.
- Most damage goes out from the shed's flank — standing in open sand is how runs end.

Bring the [SMG with a compensator ($600)](/weapons/best-weapons) — accuracy matters more than raw fire rate here. Keep the **boss meat** after the kill; the [final island route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) grills it in the volcano for endgame healing stock.
