Desert Island is where the rare-fish hunt begins. Six common species swim here alongside three rares, the Blue Shark makes its first appearance as a mini-boss, and the island hides the game's most committed Easter egg — an ice fish you summon with a $350 coconut.

## Bait

Desert Island fish bite on the **pink soap bait** — odd name, functional smell, mandatory for the island's species list.

## Fish Species

- Catfish
- Clownfish
- Yellow Boxfish
- Angelfish
- Bluegill
- Sea Urchin

**Rare fish:**

- Needlefish
- Seahorse
- Tank Fish

The rares matter beyond the collection log: any one of them is the currency for this island's boss summon.

## Mini-Boss — Blue Shark

The Blue Shark is a gear check in fish form. On the [speedrun route](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) the rule is strict: **if a Blue Shark shows up before you own the SMG, do not fight it** — reset instead. With an SMG in hand, it dies fast and sells well. Speedrunners intentionally skip handing the shark to any NPC so the grill unlock stays avoided.

## Boss — Pufferfish

The Pufferfish summon trades on the island's rare spawns:

1. Catch **any rare fish** (Needlefish, Seahorse, or Tank Fish).
2. Give it to the **tourist NPC** sitting under the tree by the shore.
3. Receive the **carrot bait** — yes, carrot — and cast it to summon the Pufferfish.

The fight is a straight shooting exam, and the [SMG](/weapons/best-weapons) with an extended magazine clears it comfortably. Winning here unlocks the route to Rock Island.

## Easter Egg — the Bing Ice Fish

Desert Island also hides a bonus fish for collectors:

1. Climb the coconut tree at the center of the island.
2. Buy the **coconut ($350)** waiting at the top.
3. Use the coconut as bait.

The result is **Bing**, an ice-themed Easter egg fish that counts as one of the game's strangest catch conditions — and one of the priciest pieces of bait in the run.
