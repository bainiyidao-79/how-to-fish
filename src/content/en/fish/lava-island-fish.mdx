Lava Island is the end of the line: three deep-sea oddities, two rares, the Goblin Shark circling as mini-boss, and the Bowhead Whale — whose defeat starts the sequence that actually ends the game.

## Bait

Lava Island fish bite on the **hextech bait**, the top tier in the game. The shop on this island also stocks the **science bait** used for the endgame boss chain — buy at least one.

## Fish Species

- Anglerfish
- Blobfish
- Stonefish

**Rare fish:**

- Oarfish
- Mini Fish

The Oarfish in particular is one of the game's showpiece catches — and worth landing before the endgame consumes your shopping list.

## Mini-Boss — Goblin Shark

The Goblin Shark is the island's mini-boss and, for completionists, a mandatory stop: it is also the species behind the [Rich! Millionaire achievement](/achievements/rich-millionaire-achievement), since a cooked Goblin Shark is the fish that funds the casino's $100,000 roulette payout. Kill it, cook it, and park it for the achievement run if money is the goal.

## Boss — Bowhead Whale

The summon is an NPC trade with a specific price:

1. Collect **five of any fish** — ground snails appear to work as well, but seagulls seem to be off the menu.
2. Hand them to the **yellow-masked NPC** to receive the **fish bait bucket**.
3. Cast it to summon the **Bowhead Whale**.

This is a two-phase boss disguised as one. Winning the whale fight is not the end — carry the corpse up the **rotating plank path on the volcano's right side** and throw it into the crater lava. That summons the true final boss: the **Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale**. The full sequence, including prep and the failure budget, is covered in the [final boss guide](/fish/bowhead-whale-final-boss).
