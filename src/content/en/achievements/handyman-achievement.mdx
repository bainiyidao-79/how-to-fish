**Handyman** is the achievement that makes the final boss fight twice as long and entirely more personal: the unlock condition is **defeating the final boss with your bare hands**. The good news, before you closes the settings and sells the SMG: only the killing blow has to be a fist.

## Unlock Condition

Official description: **defeat the final boss with your bare hands.** The final boss is the **Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale** — the second-phase whale summoned by throwing the Bowhead Whale's corpse into the volcano crater (full sequence in the [final boss guide](/fish/bowhead-whale-final-boss)).

## How to Get It

The source guide's method removes all heroics from the attempt:

1. **Run the normal endgame route.** Summon the Bowhead Whale, win phase one, carry the corpse up the plank path, and start the Mutant Lava Bowhead Whale fight exactly as usual.
2. **Lower the boss's HP with your weapons** — the upgraded [SMG](/weapons/best-weapons) does this fastest. No rule here says the whole fight is fists.
3. **Switch to your fists for the killing blow.** As the whale's health bar bottoms out, close distance and finish it bare-handed.

The achievement checks the finishing damage source, so guns-then-fists counts.

## Prep Notes

- Keep your **healing stock** from the normal route — grilled fish and snails. A fist-only finale means standing inside the danger zone longer than a gun kill.
- The fight's **one-death budget** across both whale phases still applies from the speedrun route; attempt Handyman on a run where phase one went clean.
- You keep the normal ending: whale tail to the scientist, speedboat key, credits — just with significantly more bruised knuckles than the standard run.
