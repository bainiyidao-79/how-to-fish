**I'm the bird now** is the How to Fish achievement everyone attempts the moment they hear it exists: the unlock condition is to **make the boat fly**. Not sail — fly. The solution is exactly as dignified as the achievement title suggests, and it costs $25.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/achievements/im-the-bird-now-achievement.webp" alt="The volcano glowing on Lava Island in How to Fish" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Explosives and watercraft — a natural pairing, according to this achievement.</figcaption></div>

## Unlock Condition

Official description: **make the boat fly.** The game tracks the boat getting airborne by violent means, and there is exactly one practical way to manage that.

## How to Get It

The full recipe from the source guide:

1. **Buy dynamite** — $25 at the shop (the [Island 2 shop](/tips/dynamite-guide) is the earliest source).
2. **Place the dynamite directly beside your boat.**
3. **Shoot the dynamite with a gun.**
4. The explosion launches the boat into the air — and the achievement unlocks.

That is the whole achievement. No special island, no boss prerequisite, no timing window.

## Practical Notes

- Do this **between fishing sessions**, not during one: the blast will not help your catch count, and the boat drifting mid-ocean is a worse launch pad than a beached or anchored one.
- You need **one gun** — any of them works. If you are achievement hunting early, the [Island 2 pistol](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) is enough.
- Buy **two sticks** of dynamite if you want a retry in the same trip — placement is forgiving, but the first throw of any new mechanic is a free lesson.
- If you are touring the achievement list, this pairs naturally with [Everyone's dream](/achievements/everyones-dream-achievement) — same shop, same explosive, different target.
