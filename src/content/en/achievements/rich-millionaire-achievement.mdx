**Rich! Millionaire** is the money achievement with a trap in its name. The unlock condition is not owning 100,000 — it is **selling a single item worth 100,000 or more**. No fish in the ocean carries that price tag, which is why every route to this achievement runs through one fish and one roulette spin.

## Unlock Condition

Official description: **sell something worth 100,000 or more.** The source guide is explicit about the trap: this is **one sale at one hundred thousand**, not a wallet total. Stacking small sales forever gets you rich and leaves the achievement locked.

## How to Get It

The known route needs two ingredients — the right fish and the casino's biggest multiplier:

1. **Catch the fish.** The target species has a base price around **1,906** — the [Goblin Shark from Lava Island](/fish/lava-island-fish) is the fish that fits this route.
2. **Cook it.** Grilling raises its value to roughly **2,858**.
3. **Take it to the casino and bet it on green.** A successful **green roulette bet pays 35x**.
4. **Win once.** 2,858 × 35 = **100,030** — just over the line, one spin, achievement unlocked.

## The Math, Honestly Stated

The route has a failure mode worth respecting: **green is a long shot**. The 35x payout exists precisely because it does not land often, and the fish you wager is gone on a losing spin. Two practical approaches:

- **Farm shark, spin repeatly.** The [Goblin Shark mini-boss](/fish/lava-island-fish) respawns — catch, cook, bet, repeat until green lands. Treat each attempt as 2,858 of venture capital.
- **Time it with a money run.** A standard [all-islands walkthrough](/walkthrough/all-islands-walkthrough) pass with hip-fire finish multipliers leaves plenty of margin for a few losing spins before the winning one.

## If You Only Care About the Money

Not chasing the achievement? The same loop still prints: Goblin Sharks, cooking, and the casino table are the highest-yield economy in the game once islands are open. The achievement just gives the loop a finish line.
