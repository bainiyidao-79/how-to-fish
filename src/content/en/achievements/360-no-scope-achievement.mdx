**360 no scope** is How to Fish's tribute to every trick-shot montage ever uploaded: the unlock condition is a **kill completed during a full 360-degree spin, without aiming down sights**. It sounds like a luck check. It is not — the rotation mechanics are forgiving, and there is a co-op shortcut the source guide volunteers for free.

## Unlock Condition

Official description: **kill a creature with a 360 no scope.** Two hard rules: the full **360-degree rotation happens before the shot**, and the kill is **hip-fire only** — touching the aim button voids the attempt.

## How to Get It

The mechanics, per the source guide:

1. **Complete the full 360 spin before firing.** The rotation can be split across **multiple mouse movements** — it does not need to be one glorious flick.
2. **Low sensitivity is your friend.** At lower settings, two quick swipes complete the rotation with comfortable accuracy, and the margin for error is generous.
3. **Shoot mid-spin, hip-fire only.** The kill registering while the spin is in progress is what counts.
4. **Use a low-tier bait fish as the target.** Ensure a one-shot kill — the cheapest shore fish exist precisely to be style-achievement statistics.

## The Co-op Shortcut

The source guide's tip section says the quiet part loud: **killing a downed teammate also counts.** Lower a friend's health, have them stay down, then spin-hip-fire the finishing shot. It is faster, more reliable, and entirely in the spirit of a game about shooting fish with rifles.
