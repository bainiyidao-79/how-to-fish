**Impressive** is How to Fish's style-check achievement: unlock condition is a **5x kill score multiplier**, which means the game grades how stylishly you finish a kill, not whether you finish it. The multiplier climbs with the same habits that make money fast in this game, so this achievement tends to fall out of good play — if you chase it deliberately.

<div class="art-ph"><img src="/images/achievements/impressive-achievement.webp" alt="Damage numbers popping over a whale boss in How to Fish" loading="eager" /><figcaption>Big numbers, bigger multiplier — Impressive rewards finishing fights with flair.</figcaption></div>

## Unlock Condition

Official description: **get a 5x kill score multiplier.** The multiplier builds from how you land the kill — unaimed shots, movement, and long-distance accuracy all push it up.

## How to Get It

The source guide's route, in order of reliability:

- **Ditch the scope.** Hip-fire kills carry more style weight than aiming down sights — this is the same rule that makes hip-fire finishes pay better at sale time, so the habit doubles your income.
- **Jump headshots.** Add a jump to the kill shot, aim for the head, and the multiplier stacks faster. Any target works — fish, mini-bosses, whatever is convenient.
- **The seagull long shot.** The flashiest route: headshot a **seagull in mid-flight at long range**. Seagulls cross the islands constantly, and one clean aerial hit from distance stamps the multiplier in a single shot.

## Make It Easy on Yourself

- Practice on **low-threat targets** — shore fish and passing seagulls — rather than mid-boss-fight where dying resets the attempt.
- Use a weapon you can control unscoped; the [SMG's fire rate](/weapons/best-weapons) compensates for wild hip-fire aim.
- The 360 no scope achievement ([full guide here](/achievements/360-no-scope-achievement)) stacks naturally with this one — both reward unscoped kills, so chase them in the same session.
