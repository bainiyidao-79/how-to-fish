The achievement is called **Everyone's dream**, and its official description explains why: **kill a seagull with dynamite.** Somewhere in development, Dazed Games looked at the seagulls that steal your bait, bully your shorelines, and generally conduct themselves terribly — and made their demolition the most honest achievement in the game.

## Unlock Condition

Official description: **kill a seagull with dynamite.** Shooting the seagull does not count. The dynamite has to be the killer — which is why this achievement is a trap-building exercise.

## How to Get It

The source guide's method builds a proper bait trap:

1. **Place dynamite and fish on the ground together** — the fish is the lure, the dynamite is the punchline.
2. **Back away** to a safe distance. You are not the deliverer; you are the trigger.
3. **Wait for the seagull to dive** for the fish. Seagulls patrol every island, and a free meal on the sand draws them in fast.
4. **Shoot the dynamite.** The blast takes the seagull with it, and the achievement unlocks.

## Practical Notes

- Shopping list: **one dynamite ($25)** and **one piece of fish** — the same [Island 2 shop counter](/tips/dynamite-guide) supplies both sides of the trap.
- Patience is the whole skill. Seagulls arrive on their own schedule; re-bait and wait rather than chasing them with the charge.
- Do not stand near the rig when you shoot it — the blast does not distinguish between achievement hunters and their targets.
- This pairs naturally with [I'm the bird now](/achievements/im-the-bird-now-achievement), the boat-launch achievement — same shop, same explosive, one shopping trip covers both.
